## 24-hour audit retention: exact guarantees and limits

All application administrative/moderation events use existing app_private.access_audit. Client roles have no direct write/delete/truncate access. Trigger also refuses UPDATE, TRUNCATE and deletion younger than 24 hours. No delete control in UI. Expired records are excluded at server read time, even if cleanup is delayed. Schedule `supabase/enable_moderation_retention.sql` with trusted operator AFTER enabling pg_cron. Minute cleanup physically removes records with age >=24h; a separate job removes this scheduler's old execution records. No export, file logging or secondary copy of event payloads is introduced. Under normal scheduling physical deletion occurs between 24h and 24h+one scheduling interval; outages can delay it. EXACT per-record physical deletion at the 24h instant is not guaranteed by PostgreSQL/cron.

This does not control Supabase infrastructure Auth/Postgres gateway logs, WAL, PITR, backups, replicas, provider retention or screenshots/copied data. Configure provider retention separately; physical recovery copies can outlive primary table rows. A database superuser can disable triggers; “cannot delete” applies to application admins/moderators, not a claim that an infrastructure owner is technically powerless. Complaint bodies/cases and active sanctions are operational records, not 24-hour log copies; current migration retains them. Full privacy/closed-case lifecycle needs a separate reviewed policy, not destruction of active reports after a day.
